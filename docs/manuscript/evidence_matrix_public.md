## Lesson 7 — Successful distributed computation does not imply valid evaluation

| ID | Date | Phase | Site | Symptom | Root cause | Impact | Preventive change | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | 2026-09 | consortium | UMCU, CAM | Per-site table ranked one centre worst on macro AUROC | Macro AUROC averaged a benign one-vs-rest figure computed from 2 positive cases; the ordering tracked benign support almost exactly | A centre was described as underperforming on a split artefact. Excluding that class it moves 0.602 to 0.717, another 0.710 to 0.908 | Every metric travels with its per-class support; no ranking on macro where a class has single-digit support | A summary statistic computed over a class that barely exists is not a measurement |
| E3 | 2026-09 | consortium | — | Pooled accuracy 0.752 alongside malignant AUROC 0.887 looked acceptable | Accuracy hides the operating point; the threshold was not where the AUROC suggested | A model with poor malignant recall reads as adequate | Report malignant recall alongside accuracy | Discrimination and calibration are different questions and accuracy answers neither |
| O6 | 2026-09-09 | analysis | CAM, RUMC, UMCU | Four of eight recorded site join dates were wrong by ~6 weeks | April records came from internal test machines running under hospital site names on pre-release kits | A published chart misstated when the consortium formed | Site attribution now resolved from heartbeat.json hostname, not the configured site name | A site name in a config is a label, not an identity |
| O7 | 2026-09-09 | analysis | VHIO | A site's benign count was rendered as blank rather than zero | Class absent from the counts dict was treated as missing data | A real finding (one site holds no benign cases at all) read as a gap in the record | Absent class recorded as 0, not blank | Absence of a class is data; absence of a record is not |
| O9 | 2026-09-10 | analysis | — | Uncertainty sampling found FEWER malignant cases than random at every labelling budget | Accuracy does not rise with confidence at all: the 0.5-0.6 confidence bin is 0.786 accurate while the more confident 0.6-0.7 bin is 0.250 (z=3.48, p=0.0005).
